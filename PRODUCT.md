# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: technical recruiters and hiring engineers evaluating the author's work. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or GitHub link, spend a few minutes, and need to judge two things quickly: can this person build a real ML system, and can they ship a polished full-stack product around it.

Secondary: football fans who want the model's take on the upcoming round in their league, in their own language.

## Product Purpose

A multi-league football match predictor. A Python pipeline ingests historical results, trains a probabilistic model, and publishes win/draw/loss probabilities and expected scorelines for upcoming fixtures in six leagues. The site presents those predictions alongside an honest, out-of-sample track record.

Success: a recruiter leaves understanding what the model does, how well it performs against a fair benchmark, and how the system is engineered end to end.

## Positioning

The model never sees bookmaker odds. Odds are used only as the benchmark it is scored against, so every number on the site is an honest "model vs. market" comparison rather than a repackaged betting line. Predictions shown for past matches are genuinely out of sample (produced only from data available before kick-off).

## Operating Context

- Leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Brasileirão Série A.
- Data: public football-data.co.uk CSVs (results, match stats where available, closing odds, upcoming fixtures). No API keys required.
- Delivery: a scheduled CI job runs the pipeline and commits versioned static JSON into the frontend; the static site rebuilds on commit. No database, no secrets in the browser.
- Live at oddsless.netlify.app. Previously "La Liga Predictor", a single-league, Supabase-backed app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Outputs per fixture: home/draw/away probabilities, expected goals, most likely scoreline, market implied probabilities for comparison.
- Track record: per-league season-to-date accuracy, log loss and ranked probability score versus the market and a naive baseline; multi-season walk-forward backtest.
- Brazil data lacks shot statistics; the model must handle missing features.
- Interface languages: English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Portuguese (one per covered league).
- Open: custom domain and final product name beyond the repository name.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real data only: every prediction, metric and chart is generated by the pipeline. No invented accuracy claims, testimonials or user counts.
- Existing assets: `GitVisuals/LaLigaDemo.gif` (old UI demo, to be replaced), `frontend/public/LaLigaLogo.png` (league logo, legacy).

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over hype: show calibration and losses against the market as plainly as wins.
2. Every number is reproducible from the repository.
3. Fixtures first, depth on demand: the upcoming round is the front door; method and evaluation are one step away.
4. Engineering is part of the product: the pipeline, tests and CI are presented, not hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Outcome encoding never relies on colour alone. Fully localized UI, dates and numbers in six languages.
